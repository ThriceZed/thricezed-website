1. The Agreement

ThriceZed and the Client agree the details of each booking directly, including the package selected, the events to be covered, the dates and locations, the approximate video length, the total fee, and the delivery turnaround. Those agreed booking details, together with these terms, form the whole agreement for that booking.

Where a booking detail and these terms conflict, the detail agreed for that specific booking controls for that booking only. Everything else in these terms continues to apply.

2. Services and Deliverables

ThriceZed will attend the events agreed at booking, record video footage, and produce an edited highlight video of approximately the agreed length (the "Deliverable"). Creative decisions, including shot selection, pacing, music, color, and final edit, rest with ThriceZed, subject to the revision process in Section 7.

The Deliverable is provided by digital delivery. Any additional services, including extra video length, effects or CGI scenes, additional games, or rush delivery, are billed at the rates published by ThriceZed and must be agreed in advance.

3. Fees and Payment

  1. The total fee is the amount agreed at booking.
  2. Where a deposit is agreed, it is non-refundable, and the booking is not confirmed and no date is held until the deposit is received.
  3. The remaining balance is due before the Deliverable is released, unless otherwise agreed.
  4. ThriceZed may withhold delivery of any Deliverable while any amount remains unpaid.
  5. Travel beyond the standard service area, where applicable, is billed as agreed at booking.

4. Scheduling, Cancellation, and Postponement

  1. If the Client cancels more than seven (7) days before a scheduled event, any amounts paid beyond the deposit are refunded. The deposit is not refundable.
  2. If the Client cancels within seven (7) days of a scheduled event, the full fee for that event remains due.
  3. If an event is postponed, cancelled, or shortened by the school, league, venue, officials, or weather, ThriceZed will make reasonable efforts to reschedule at no additional service fee. If rescheduling is not possible, the parties will agree on a substitute event or a partial refund of amounts attributable to the missed event, less the deposit.
  4. The Client is responsible for giving ThriceZed accurate event dates, times, and locations, and for notifying ThriceZed promptly of any change.

ThriceZed provides videography and editing services only. The Client acknowledges and agrees that:

  2. The content of the Deliverable depends entirely on what occurs during the recorded event. ThriceZed makes no representation, warranty, or guarantee as to the athlete's athletic performance, the amount of playing time received, or the number or quality of highlight-worthy plays available to capture.
  3. ThriceZed makes no representation, warranty, or guarantee that any Deliverable will result in athletic recruitment, scholarship offers, roster selection, sponsorship, media attention, or any particular level of social media engagement or audience response.
  4. Fees are paid for the services performed, not for any particular result. No refund, discount, credit, or re-shoot is owed on the basis that the Client or the athlete is dissatisfied with the athlete's own athletic performance, playing time, or the events that occurred during the recorded event.

6. ThriceZed Service Failures

Section 5 addresses matters outside the control of ThriceZed. This section addresses matters within its control. If ThriceZed fails to attend a scheduled event, or if footage is unusable because of equipment failure, operator error, or data loss attributable to ThriceZed, then ThriceZed will, at the Client's election:

  1. Record a substitute event of comparable scope at no additional service fee; or
  2. Refund the portion of the fee attributable to the affected event.

This section states the Client's exclusive remedy for a service failure by ThriceZed.

7. Delivery, Review, and Revisions

  1. ThriceZed will deliver the Deliverable within the turnaround agreed at booking, measured from the later of the final recorded event or receipt of full payment.
  2. The Client may request one (1) round of reasonable revisions within seven (7) days of delivery. Revisions are limited to adjustments such as clip selection, trimming, ordering, and text corrections.
  3. Requests that change the agreed scope, including added length, added events, or new effects work, are additional services and are billed accordingly.
  4. If the Client does not request revisions within seven (7) days, the Deliverable is deemed accepted.
